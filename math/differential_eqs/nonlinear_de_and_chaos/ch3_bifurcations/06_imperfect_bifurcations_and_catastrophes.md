# Imperfect Bifurcations and Catastrophes

**Normal Form:** $\dot{x} = h + rx - x^3$ (Imperfect bifurcation)
$h$: imperfection parameter.

- Like a pitchfork bifurcation, but with an offset $h$ that can cause disconnected trajectories
- Stability Diagrams and Parameter space are great tools used to map behaviors when working with this kind of bifurcation

**Cusp Catastrophe Surface:** A projection onto the parameters, a cross-section gives a bifurcation diagram of the other variable. It is termed a catastrophe because of sudden state change with parameter change (Fall).
