
**Course Description:** Notes from "Nonlinear Differential Equations and Chaos" (3rd Edition) by Steven H. Strogatz, including: Fundamentals of vector fields on the line, fixed point stability, and one-parameter bifurcations (saddle-node, transcritical, and pitchfork). Phase plane analysis, linear and non-linear systems, limit cycles, coupled oscillators, and two-dimensional bifurcations like the Hopf bifurcation. Chaotic dynamics, the Lorenz equations, strange attractors, 1D maps, period-doubling routes to chaos, and fractal geometry.
